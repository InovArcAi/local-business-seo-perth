What discovery looks like

Discovery answers one question: why is this business not getting the enquiries its market could give it. Search Scope reviews the Google Business Profile, the website's technical health, the content, the link profile and the competitors who currently hold the map results in each commercially meaningful suburb.

Local visibility comes down to relevance, prominence and proximity, and the review is honest about proximity from the start. One location cannot rank across an entire metro area, so the plan gets built around the territory the business can realistically win rather than the whole city. Discovery also records how enquiries arrive today, because calls and form submissions are the numbers the whole engagement will be judged on.

The output is a prioritised plan, not a menu. A business owner should finish discovery knowing which handful of changes will move enquiries and which popular tactics would waste six months.

What month one contains

Month one is audit, access and foundation. The audit runs across the Google Business Profile, on-page signals, technical health, the link profile and AI citations. Analytics and Search Console access is set up in the client's own name, so the client owns its data from the first week and keeps it whatever happens later. Conversion tracking gets wired to calls and forms before any optimisation starts, because improvement claims mean nothing without a baseline.

The back half of the month goes into the technical foundation and a top-to-bottom rebuild of the Google Business Profile. New pages and authority work wait until that base is solid, which is deliberate: shipping content onto a broken foundation just produces more pages that underperform.

How months two and three build

Months two and three turn the audit into shipped work. Suburb and service pages get built only where demand justifies them, tiered by commercial value rather than templated across the whole metro, because Google treats a page-per-suburb spin-out as doorway pages and quietly declines to rank them.

Authority building starts in the same window, alongside content written to be cited by AI answer engines as well as ranked in classic results. Geo-grid rank tracking runs from this phase onward and measures positions across the suburbs the business serves, instead of a single postcode check that flatters or hides the real picture.

How ongoing months run

Ongoing months follow a steady loop: review the numbers, pick the highest-value work, ship it, measure what moved. The mix shifts with what the data shows. Some months lean on content, others on the Google Business Profile, listing consistency or authority building.

Because the same person runs the strategy and the delivery, the plan can change mid-month when the data says it should, without a change request working its way through an account team.

How reporting works

Reporting is a plain-English monthly report covering three things: the work completed, the movement in rankings and enquiries, and the plan for the month ahead. Positions come from geo-grid tracking across the suburbs that matter, and enquiry numbers come from the call and form tracking wired up in month one, so the report reflects business outcomes rather than vanity metrics.

The reporting is honest in both directions. What moved gets reported, and so does what did not, along with what will change because of it.

How the engagement ends

The engagement runs month-to-month with no lock-in contracts, and either side can close it with 30 days notice. That structure is deliberate. It keeps the incentive pointed the right way: the work has to keep earning the relationship every month, rather than a signed term doing the earning.

Leaving is clean because everything was set up in the client's name from month one. The analytics, the Search Console property, the Google Business Profile and the conversion tracking all stay with the business, and the final month goes to handover rather than negotiation.

Frequently asked questions

What does an SEO consultant actually do?

An SEO consultant works out why a business is not visible, decides which fixes matter in which order, then either does the work or directs the people who will. Judgement is the product. Of thirty possible improvements on a given site, a handful will actually move enquiries, and knowing which ones saves months of wasted activity. The mix usually spans technical diagnosis, site and location architecture, content direction, authority building, Google Business Profile strategy and AI search visibility, weighted towards whatever is actually broken.

SEO consultant vs SEO agency, which is better?

Neither wins outright, because they solve different problems. A consultant fits when direction is the gap: results have stalled and nobody can explain why, or the previous supplier produced activity without outcomes. An agency fits when the strategy is settled and the gap is delivery capacity across content, links and production. Some businesses run both, with a consultant setting direction and an agency executing it. Search Scope sits on the consultant side of that line, with the founder handling delivery.

What does month one actually look like?

Month one is audit, access and foundation. The first half covers a full audit spanning the Google Business Profile, on-page signals, technical health, the link profile and AI citations, along with analytics and Search Console access set up in the client's own name and conversion tracking wired to calls and forms. The second half starts repairing the technical foundation and rebuilding the Google Business Profile from top to bottom. Pages and authority work begin once that base is sound.

How is Search Scope different from a typical Perth SEO agency?

Three things separate the two. Delivery is founder-led, so the person a client speaks to at the start is the person inside the accounts every month, not a junior handed the file after signing. The focus is narrow, covering SEO and AI search only, with no web design, social or paid ads competing for attention. And the terms are month-to-month with 30 days notice, which means the work has to keep earning the engagement instead of a contract holding it in place.
